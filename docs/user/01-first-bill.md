# Quick Desk Reference: First Bill & Counter POS Operations
**Document ID:** SOP-POS-001 | **Target:** Cashiers & Dispensing Pharmacists

---

## 1. Fast Keyboarding Workflow
| Step | Action | Hotkey |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Focus Terminal** | Open POS billing screen | `F2` |
| **2. Search Formulation** | Brand name, generic molecule, or EAN barcode | `Alt + S` |
| **3. Batch Selection** | Nearest non-expired batch auto-selected by FEFO | `Arrow Down` $\rightarrow$ `Enter` |
| **4. Customer Mobile** | Input 10-digit mobile number for loyalty & GST invoice | `Alt + C` |
| **5. Doctor Reg #** | Mandatory if formulation is Schedule H or H1 | Tab into Doctor field |
| **6. Park Current Cart** | Put cart on hold to serve next waiting patient | `F8` |
| **7. Recall Held Cart** | Resume parked customer bill | `Alt + P` |
| **8. Checkout & Print** | Split payment (Cash/UPI), finalize, and print 80mm bill | `F4` or `Ctrl + Enter` |

---

## 2. Statutory Prescription Compliance (D&C Rules)
- **Schedule H / H1 Flag:** Formulations containing antibiotics, narcotics, or habit-forming molecules trigger the statutory prescription modal.
- **Mandatory Fields:** Doctor Registration Number, Prescriber Clinic Name, Patient Full Name & City.
- **Form 65(9)(b) Register:** Automatically committed to the immutable register upon invoice creation.

---

## 3. Thermal Printer Checklist (80mm ESC/POS)
1. Verify green power light on the desktop thermal printer.
2. If paper jams, press the manual cut lever, clear paper, and reprint from **Sales History (`Alt + H`)**.
3. Duplicate bills clearly carry the heading `"DUPLICATE TAX INVOICE"`.
