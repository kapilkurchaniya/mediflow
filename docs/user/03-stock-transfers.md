# Quick Desk Reference: Inter-Branch Stock Transfers & E-Way Bills
**Document ID:** SOP-INV-003 | **Target:** Warehouse Staff & Branch Managers

---

## 1. Creating a Transfer Indent
1. Go to **Inventory $\rightarrow$ Transfers $\rightarrow$ New Transfer** (`/dashboard/inventory/transfers/new`).
2. Choose **Source Store** (Issuing branch/warehouse) and **Destination Store** (Receiving retail branch).
3. Add medicines and required quantities. MediFlow shows available shelf batches and expiry dates at the source.
4. Save and Submit for Dispatch.

---

## 2. Dispatch & GST Rule 55 Delivery Challan
- When packing stock for courier/van transport, print the **Statutory Delivery Challan**.
- If consignment total value exceeds **₹50,000**, input Vehicle Number or Transporter ID to generate **Part-A of the E-Way Bill**.

---

## 3. Inward Receiving & Variance Resolution
- Destination store opens the incoming transfer in MediFlow and scans received physical boxes.
- **Match:** Local batch stock increments automatically.
- **Shortage / Breakage:** Staff marks damaged/missing units. MediFlow logs a transit variance claim against the carrier or issuing store.
